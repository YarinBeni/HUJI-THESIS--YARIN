# Review pack — the SSL scale-up, 2026-09-02 → 09-04

For a reader who knows the M.Sc. thesis and nothing else on this branch.
Everything here lives on `yarin-sandbox`; the numbered `C*` jobs are cluster
runs, each with its log under `chrono/reports/logs/`.

## The question

The M.Sc. showed that LLM features date **named entities** well (ρ ≈ .88) and
**documents** poorly. The PhD plan's answer was to learn a chronological
representation without labels, from the large unlabelled Akkadian corpora, and
then use the 1,193 dated royal inscriptions only for the final read-out.

This wave tested exactly that claim.

## What was built

**S0 — one corpus (`make_ssl_corpus.py`).** 31,905 Akkadian texts from six
sources: eBL 16,552 · ORACC 11,268 · Archibab 1,522 · Late-Babylonian letters
1,020 · **ORCC 1,185 — the dated royal inscriptions, the test set** · SEAL 358.
Split so that every SSL run trains on `split == "train"` only; no run ever saw
a dated inscription.

> **Data bug found on the way (`STATUS.md` DATA-3).** The unified corpus held
> 574,744 duplicate ORACC word rows. The true census is ≈ 1.88 M words, not the
> 2.45 M quoted in `justification/data_source_summary.md` and
> `v_1/data/README.md`. **That claim in the M.Sc. documentation needs
> correcting before any thesis text repeats it.**

**S2 — 32 self-supervised runs, ~100 GPU-hours**, all on the unlabelled split:

| family | what it is | runs |
|---|---|---|
| adapter | small MLP on a frozen encoder's document vector | 16 (4 objectives × 4 encoders) |
| from scratch | a Transformer over raw signs, no LLM underneath | 8 (S 9.4M / M 23.6M / L 50.5M / XL 104.5M × 2 objectives) |
| hybrid | frozen encoder's TOKEN states → a fresh Transformer | 8 (2 encoders × S/M × 2 objectives) |

Objectives: Barlow Twins, BYOL, JEPA, InfoNCE. Views (augmentations): crop to
16/32 words, drop a ~10 % span, orthographic normalisation, random sign
masking — generated on the fly.

**S3 — 66 supervised fine-tunes** on the dated set: SSL init vs. a random-init
control, label fractions 25/50/100 %, head widths 128/512/2048, 3 seeds.

## The read-out that counts

`EMIN_SSL_RESULT.md` (C18) scores **every** representation on the M.Sc.
experiment itself: the 1,193 dated inscriptions, ruler-grouped folds, ridge on
frozen features, SLA §7 (mc_balanced draws + the gkf pooled ρ). Only the
representation changes between rows.

**Harness check:** `cuneiformBase-400m::L12` reads mc **.448** here against the
**.45 ± .02** published as E-MIN v2's ridge baseline. The protocol reproduces.

| family | cells | best mc ρ | best gkf ρ | median gkf ρ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **frozen encoder, no SSL** | 6 | .448 | **.419** | **.312** |
| adapter on a frozen encoder | 16 | .494 | .393 | .277 |
| from scratch (raw signs) | 8 | .403 | .333 | .237 |
| hybrid (frozen → fresh Transformer) | 8 | .386 | .294 | .171 |
| *supervised Chrono-Barlow head (E-MIN v2)* | — | *.61* | — | — |

## Conclusions

1. **Self-supervised pretraining on 31,905 unlabelled Akkadian texts does not
   improve dating.** No family beats the frozen encoder on the pooled read-out,
   on best or on median. The hybrid variant is the weakest of the three.
2. **The likely reason is the augmentations.** Cropping, span-dropping and
   masking teach *invariance to cropping*; the cheapest feature that satisfies
   them is corpus identity, not time. The source probe reads .92–.98 in every
   cell (`SCALING_RESULT.md`) — the representations know which corpus a text
   came from almost perfectly.
3. **Period accuracy is not evidence about dating here.** Period is nearly a
   function of source (Old Babylonian = Archibab, Late Babylonian = the
   letters, Hellenistic = ORACC), so the .81–.90 period probes are close to
   restating the source probe. This is why the read-out moved to the thesis
   protocol.
4. **1,193 labels are worth more than 31,905 unlabelled texts**: the supervised
   head's .61 is untouched by anything here.
5. **The small domain encoder wins.** cuneiformBase-400m beats Qwen3-8B and
   Llama-2-7B throughout — the same ordering as E-MIN v2.
6. **One place SSL did help** (`S3_RESULT.md`): at **25 % labels**, Barlow init
   gives **+.066** gkf ρ over the random-init control (+.028 for JEPA); at 50 %
   and 100 % the effect is gone. Suggestive, not established — sd is .02–.06
   and there are 3 seeds.

## Errors made and corrected, for the record

- **A broken transfer test, and a retracted claim.** The first held-out test
  scored balanced accuracy over the periods shared between the undated pool and
  the dated inscriptions: Neo-Assyrian (924 test docs), Middle Babylonian (28)
  and Hellenistic (**one**), while the training pool holds 5 Neo-Babylonian and
  52 Middle Babylonian texts. It read .10–.20, "below chance", and I first
  reported that as evidence of an anti-chronological representation. It is an
  artefact of the class filter. Retracted in `SCALING_RESULT.md`, and replaced
  by C17 (continuous) and C18 (the thesis protocol).
- **30 GPU-hours nearly lost.** Six of the eight hybrid runs trained their full
  five hours and were then killed at the 6:30 wall limit *during* the embedding
  pass (embedding 31,905 texts through a frozen 7B encoder takes over an hour).
  They resume from checkpoints; rescued with a 6-minute budget. The training
  budget is now separate from the wall limit.
- **An 8-hour push outage.** A job copied 37–400 MB checkpoints into
  `chrono/reports/`, every later commit swept them along, and `pack-objects`
  was OOM-killed on the login node, so no results reached the repository for
  eight hours. `commit_push_sandbox` now refuses any file over 20 MB.
